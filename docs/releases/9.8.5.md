# Memozia 9.8.5 (165)

- In **Settings → About → Feedback**, choose the app that should open your email draft. The list comes from macOS and includes the apps registered to handle email links on your Mac. Mail clients appear before browsers.
- **Open Email Draft** opens the selected app with the recipient, subject, and feedback filled in. Your feedback stays in Memozia until you close the form, so you can try another app if a compose window does not appear. Review and send the message in your mail app.

This unnotarized preview runs on Apple Silicon (arm64) with macOS 15 or later. The app is ad-hoc signed with Hardened Runtime enabled. Sparkle verifies the update ZIP using Ed25519.

Memozia 9.7.0 and later can check for updates in the app. For a first installation, extract the ZIP and copy `Memozia.app` to Applications.

The macOS unit suite recorded 551 passes and seven environment-gated skips. The feedback form was exercised with Apple Mail, including the recipient, subject, body, and returning to the original form. Live installation during a recording, handoff to a running background worker, and macOS permission prompts during an in-place upgrade remain manual checks.
