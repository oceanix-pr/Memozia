# Memozia 9.8.3 (163)

- Report bugs or request features from **Settings → About → Feedback**. **Open Email Draft** fills in the subject, your message, and the address [oceanix-pr@outlook.com](mailto:oceanix-pr@outlook.com). Review the draft and send it from your email app.
- The feedback form shows a copyable address. If the email app fails to open, your text stays in the form so you can send it manually.

This unnotarized preview supports Apple Silicon (arm64) on macOS 15 or later. The app is ad-hoc signed with Hardened Runtime enabled. The update ZIP is signed with Sparkle Ed25519.

Memozia 9.7.0 and later can check for updates in the app. For a first installation or an upgrade from an earlier version, extract the ZIP and copy `Memozia.app` to Applications.
